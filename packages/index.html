Health Checkup FAQs

Common questions regarding package preparation, sample collections, and reports.

Is fasting required before taking full body health checkups?

Yes. Most comprehensive packages including Fasting Blood Glucose, Lipid Profile, and Liver Function tests require an overnight fasting of 10 to 12 hours. You may drink plain water, but avoid morning tea, coffee, milk, or heavy snacks until after blood is drawn.

How can I schedule a home sample collection in Mohali or Chandigarh?

You can book doorstep collection by clicking "Book Home Collection" on this page, calling our direct helpline at +91 75288 88060, or messaging us on WhatsApp. Our certified phlebotomist will arrive at your home at your preferred morning slot.

How quickly will I receive my test reports?

Routine parameters (CBC, LFT, KFT, Blood Sugar, Urine) are reported within 6 to 12 hours. Packages including specialized markers like HbA1c, Vitamin D3, and Vitamin B12 are delivered within 18 to 24 hours directly via WhatsApp PDF and secure online download.

Do I need a doctor's prescription to book preventive packages?

No doctor prescription is required to book any preventive or wellness health checkup package. However, if your doctor has written specific test requirements, you can simply upload your prescription photo to our WhatsApp line for instant customized booking.

Need Help Choosing the Best Package for Your Family?

Connect with our senior diagnostic coordinators in Sector 52 Chandigarh / Mohali. We will recommend the right screening based on your age, symptoms, or medical history.

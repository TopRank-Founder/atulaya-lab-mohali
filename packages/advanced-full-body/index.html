Why Full Body Testing is Crucial in Chandigarh & Mohali

Sedentary lifestyles, indoor desk jobs, and stress contribute significantly to silent Vitamin D3 deficiencies (found in over 85% of urban adults in Tricity), borderline high cholesterol, and pre-diabetes (HbA1c between 5.7% and 6.4%).

By screening liver transaminases, kidney filtration rates, and thyroid hormones simultaneously, the Atulaya Advanced Full Body Checkup helps detect lifestyle diseases years before visible symptoms develop.

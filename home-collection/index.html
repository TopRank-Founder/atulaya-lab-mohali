Patient Preparation Guidelines

  • Fasting Tests: Consume no food or beverage (except plain water) for 10–12 hours prior to fasting blood tests.
  • Hydration: Drink 1–2 glasses of normal water before sample extraction to facilitate smoother blood draw.
  • Medications: Continue prescription blood pressure or cardiac medications unless your doctor advises otherwise.

About Atulaya Healthcare Lab Mohali

Committed to diagnostic accuracy, patient empathy, and modern pathology standards to support clinical decisions across Mohali and Chandigarh.

Our Mission & Values

Reliable Diagnostics Driven by Clinical Integrity

Atulaya Healthcare Lab was established with a singular objective: to make high-quality, dependable diagnostic services easily accessible to patients, families, and healthcare providers in Mohali and the greater Tricity region.

Located conveniently in Phase-7, Sector-61/52, our laboratory operates 24 hours a day to ensure critical test parameters are delivered promptly when patients and consulting doctors need them most.

Patient-First Ethos

Every sample represents an individual's wellbeing. We ensure gentle phlebotomy, courteous assistance, and zero-compromise accuracy.

Transparent & Ethical Diagnostics

Clear test explanations, upfront pricing on all health checkup packages, and zero unnecessary hidden charges.

Core Commitments

Our Diagnostic Standards

We maintain rigorous internal quality control measures, regular analyzer calibrations, and stringent cold-chain protocols for every sample collected at our center or at patients' doorsteps.

  • Dual-level internal quality control runs every morning
  • Unique barcode tracking to eliminate sample handling errors
  • Direct digital report dispatch on WhatsApp and Email
  • Round-the-clock lab operations for urgent & routine testing
How We Deliver Precision

The Four Pillars of Atulaya Healthcare Lab

Behind every test result is a structured workflow designed to safeguard sample integrity and data accuracy.

1. Sterile & Safe Collection

From fresh single-use vacutainers to strict hand hygiene and bio-waste disposal, safety is observed at every touchpoint.

2. Cold-Chain Integrity

Samples collected during doorstep visits are immediately sealed in temperature-stabilized carriers to prevent biochemical degradation.

3. Prompt Turnaround

Automated testing systems enable rapid report generation, ensuring patients and clinicians receive vital diagnostic insights without delay.

Community Service

Serving Mohali, Chandigarh & Tricity Neighborhoods
